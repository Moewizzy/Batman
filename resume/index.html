Professional Summary

Night shift crime fighter with over twenty years of experience keeping Gotham City safe without a single superpower. Known for solving cases nobody else can crack, building custom gear in house, and showing up exactly when people need help most. Brings world class preparation, a calm head under pressure, and a firm rule against hurting anyone who can be stopped another way. Seeking a long term role protecting the innocent. Happy to work alone, but leads teams of superheroes very well.
